---
title: "fw_missions"
description: "Offer union work at a clerk, and pay the character who finishes it."
---

Runs on the server. A player reads the work on the Work tab of the store counter, next to Goods. The server checks the clerk, the satchel, the company, and whether that work was already finished. The client's ask does not grant the dollars or the standing.

`source` is the client id of the player who triggered the event. It changes every time they join. A union is a reputation class from [fw_reputation](/api/fw-reputation), such as `general`. The standing words that class already uses are Unknown, Recognized, Trusted, and Established. This resource does not keep a second score. When a mission names an unlock, it calls `Set` once, and only upward.

`nil` means the export did not do it, and the second value is the reason. `That errand is already registered.` means the id was used once. `The errands are not ready.` means the tables are not ready yet.

Ask `IsReady` first. The server can say `fw_missions` has started a moment before the tables are ready. Keep asking for about two minutes. You can also listen for `fw_missions:ready`. That event is not queued. Your script has to be running already, or you missed it.

Start it after `fw_inventory`, `fw_reputation`, `fw_stores`, `fw_money`, and `fw_group`.

```
ensure fw_inventory
ensure fw_reputation
ensure fw_stores
ensure fw_money
ensure fw_group
ensure fw_missions
```

Buying at a counter does not raise standing. `FwStoresConfig.purchasePoints` on [fw_stores](/api/fw-stores) is `0`. A tier moves when a finished mission calls `Set`.

## The shape of a mission

Call `RegisterMission` from your own resource when the mission is ready to publish. Do not edit `fw_missions` to add one. The table you pass is copied into a draft and then published. An open run keeps the copy it accepted. Changing the definition later does not change that run.

An admin can do the same work from the Missions page in [fw_admin](/api/fw-admin). Save draft stores the body, and the clerks named on it offer that draft. Publish refuses it until the fields below are filled, and the screen marks the field. After publish, a new accept uses that published copy. Disable stops new accepts. A run that is already open can still be finished. Duplicate copies the body onto a new id and leaves it as a draft.

The id is a plain word: a letter, then letters, numbers, or `_`. It can be up to 64 characters. `union` is the same kind of word, up to 32 characters, and it has to be a union that is still offered.

- `title` and `text` are what the clerk says.
- `offers` is the list of store ids that may hand the work out. Each store's type has to match the union's shop type, when the union has one.
- `requires` is earlier mission ids in the same union. A cycle is refused. Leave it empty and this one can be accepted on its own.
- `repeatable` left out, or false, means once. After they finish it, they cannot take it again. True means they can take it again after the cooldown. Leave `cooldownMinutes` empty and that wait is 24 hours. A number is the wait in minutes.
- `minStanding` is optional: `Recognized`, `Trusted`, or `Established`.
- `minGroup` is how many of the company must be online. Leave it out and it is 1. The cap is 4 unless `shared/config.lua` says otherwise. The check runs on accept, and again on the submit that finishes the work. [fw_group](/api/fw-group) counts who is online. Someone who is not in a company counts as one.
- `reward` is a whole number of dollars, or `0`. An empty reward can be saved as a draft. Publish refuses it.
- `unlockStanding` is optional. On the finish, the score is set to that word's line when their score is still below it.
- `doneText` is the line they see when it is finished.
- `stages` is the ordered work. Each stage is `supply`, `deliver`, or `introduce`.

A stage has `text`, `contact`, and `items`. `contact` is `issuer` or one store id. A supply stage is turned in at the clerk who gave the work. A deliver or an introduce stage needs the other clerk. `items` is `{ name, count, hint }`. The name has to be an item the satchel already knows. Introduce may also `grant` one mission item, such as `letter_merchants_introduction`. That item needs `mission = true` in fw_inventory. The server puts one in the satchel when that stage is current, and only if they are not already holding one.

`visit`, `recover`, and `work` are not stages. A definition that still uses them will not publish.

Accept copies the published body onto the run. A mission that has never been published copies the draft instead. Turn-in reads that copy. A later edit does not change an open run. A goods line with no item name is not asked for. Disable does not cancel an open run either.

Partial turn-in stores the counts already handed over. The client may send a quantity. The server clamps it to what is left and what the satchel holds. Submit available is that clamp. Opening the counter does not take goods. Goods in a pouch do not count.

Abandon is allowed from the journal and from Work. Handed-in goods stay handed in. A letter this mission gave is removed. One-time work can be accepted again after abandon. Complete cannot. Repeatable work can be accepted again after the cooldown, on a new copy.

The list starts empty. Add a job from the Missions page. A goods line with no item is left off the hand-in. An empty payment pays nothing. Publish still refuses that body until the payment and every item name are set. A contact that is still empty is marked when they try to publish. A later job stays on the list, and Accept stays off until the earlier one is finished.

## Exports

### IsReady

Runs on the server. Yes after the mission tables exist.

```lua
--- True when a mission can be saved.
---@return boolean
exports.fw_missions:IsReady()
```

```lua
while not exports.fw_missions:IsReady() do
    Wait(100)
end
```

### RegisterMission

Runs on the server. Stores one mission and publishes it. Yes when it was published.

False when the id is already used, or a field cannot be published. The second value is the reason. A draft may still be sitting in the list when publish fails. Fix it on the Missions page, or pick a new id.

```lua
--- Stores one mission and publishes it. False when the id is taken or a field cannot be used.
---@param definition table id, title, text, union, offers, stages, reward, and optional requires, repeatable, cooldownMinutes, minStanding, minGroup, unlockStanding, doneText
---@return boolean ok
---@return string|nil err
exports.fw_missions:RegisterMission(definition)
```

```lua
local ok, err = exports.fw_missions:RegisterMission({
    id = 'gmu_beans',
    title = 'A short order',
    text = 'The clerk is short on tins.',
    union = 'general',
    offers = { 'ValentineGeneral' },
    reward = 2,
    minGroup = 1,
    stages = {
        {
            kind = 'supply',
            text = 'Hand over the tins.',
            contact = 'issuer',
            items = { { name = 'canned_beans', count = 1, hint = 'A general store still sells them.' } },
        },
    },
})
if not ok then
    print(err)
    return
end
```

### AdminList

Runs on the server. The Missions page asks for this. Another script can call it too. Pass the admin's `source`. Nil when they do not have `fw.admin.missions`. The second value is the reason.

The table has `missions`, `unions`, `stores`, `types`, and `seasons`.

```lua
--- The lists for the Missions page. Nil when this player cannot edit missions.
---@param source integer
---@return table|nil list
---@return string|nil err
exports.fw_missions:AdminList(source)
```

### AdminRead

Runs on the server. One mission, plus the field errors a publish would raise. The same lists as `AdminList` come back with `mission` set. Nil when the id is missing or they cannot edit missions.

```lua
--- One mission and the field errors. Nil when it is missing or this player cannot edit missions.
---@param source integer
---@param id string
---@return table|nil list
---@return string|nil err
exports.fw_missions:AdminRead(source, id)
```

### AdminSave

Runs on the server. Saves a draft, including one that is not ready to publish. The id cannot change after the first save. Returns the read list. Nil when the body cannot be stored.

```lua
--- Saves a draft. Nil when the body cannot be stored.
---@param source integer
---@param body table
---@return table|nil list
---@return string|nil err
exports.fw_missions:AdminSave(source, body)
```

### AdminPublish

Runs on the server. Publishes the saved draft and appends a revision. Published revisions are not edited. Returns the read list. When a field is wrong, the list still comes back, `mission.errors` marks the fields, and the second value says to fix them. Nil when the mission is missing.

```lua
--- Publishes the saved draft. The list still comes back when a field must be fixed.
---@param source integer
---@param id string
---@return table|nil list
---@return string|nil err
exports.fw_missions:AdminPublish(source, id)
```

### AdminDisable

Runs on the server. Stops new accepts. An open run can still be finished. Returns the read list. Nil when the mission is missing.

```lua
--- Stops new accepts. Nil when the mission is missing.
---@param source integer
---@param id string
---@return table|nil list
---@return string|nil err
exports.fw_missions:AdminDisable(source, id)
```

### AdminDuplicate

Runs on the server. Copies a mission onto a new draft id. Returns the read list for the copy. Nil when the new id is already used or the source is missing.

```lua
--- Copies a mission onto a new draft id.
---@param source integer
---@param sourceId string
---@param newId string
---@return table|nil list
---@return string|nil err
exports.fw_missions:AdminDuplicate(source, sourceId, newId)
```

### AdminUnionSave

Runs on the server. Creates a union, or changes its name, shop type, and whether it is offered. The id does not change after create. An empty shop type means the union does not gate a shelf. Returns the list. Nil when the id or the name cannot be used.

This also calls [fw_reputation](/api/fw-reputation) `RegisterClass`, so the score can be read under that id.

```lua
--- Creates a union or updates its name. The id does not change after create.
---@param source integer
---@param payload table id, label, storeType, enabled
---@return table|nil list
---@return string|nil err
exports.fw_missions:AdminUnionSave(source, payload)
```

### AdminUnionRemove

Runs on the server. Deletes a union that no mission and no score still uses. When something still uses it, the union is disabled instead, the list comes back, and the second value says why. Nil when the union is missing.

```lua
--- Removes a union nothing still uses. Otherwise it is disabled and the reason comes back.
---@param source integer
---@param id string
---@return table|nil list
---@return string|nil err
exports.fw_missions:AdminUnionRemove(source, id)
```

### AdminSeasonSave

Runs on the server. Saves a season that has not run. `startsAt` and `endsAt` are unix times, and the end has to be later. `unions` names the classes to wipe. `resetStanding` and `resetMissions` default to yes. Pass `id` to update one that has not run. A season that already ran is refused. Returns the list.

```lua
--- Saves a season that has not run.
---@param source integer
---@param payload table name, startsAt, endsAt, unions, and optional id, resetStanding, resetMissions
---@return table|nil list
---@return string|nil err
exports.fw_missions:AdminSeasonSave(source, payload)
```

### AdminSeasonEnd

Runs on the server. Runs that season now. Standing for the named unions is copied, then set to 0. Mission rows for those unions are copied, then removed. Dollars and items stay. A season that already ran is refused. Returns the list. Nil when it could not run.

```lua
--- Runs a season now. Nil when it already ran or it is missing.
---@param source integer
---@param seasonId integer
---@return table|nil list
---@return string|nil err
exports.fw_missions:AdminSeasonEnd(source, seasonId)
```

## Events

### fw_missions:ready

Local. It fires on the server when the tables are ready. It is not queued. A listener that starts later has missed it. Use `IsReady` if your script may start second.

```lua
AddEventHandler('fw_missions:ready', function()
end)
```

### fw_missions:list

Networked. The counter sends it. `storeId` is the open store, such as `ValentineGeneral`. The answer comes back on `fw_missions:work` to that player only.

The server does not grant anything for a list.

### fw_missions:accept

Networked. The counter sends it when they press Accept. `storeId` is the open store. `missionId` is the mission id. They have to be at that clerk, the mission has to be published, and the company has to be large enough when `minGroup` is more than 1. The answer is `fw_missions:work`.

### fw_missions:turnIn

Networked. The counter sends it when they press Submit. `storeId` is the open store. `missionId` is the mission id. `request` may include `lines` as `{ name, count }`, and `available` as true to hand over whatever the satchel has. The server clamps the count. The answer is `fw_missions:work`.

### fw_missions:abandon

Networked. The counter or the journal sends it. `missionId` is the mission. `storeId` is set when they are at the counter, and left out from the journal. The answer is `fw_missions:work` or `fw_missions:journal` for that player only.

### fw_missions:reissue

Networked. Asks for the stage letter again when the satchel has none. Same arguments as abandon.

### fw_missions:journal

Networked. The bag sends it. The answer is `fw_missions:journal` to that player only: the work they have accepted, and the abandon policy. The server does not grant anything for a journal.

### fw_missions:request

Networked. The client sends it after this resource starts, so a mark can come back without a new character select. No reward.

### fw_missions:work

Sent to that player only. It is the list the Work tab shows. Each card has the title, the step, the lines, and whether Accept, Submit, or Abandon is available. The dollars were already decided on the server. Do not grant items because this list arrived.

### fw_missions:steps

Sent to that player only. It is the contact for the stage they are on, so their map can show a mark. Do not grant items because a mark arrived.
