title: "Characters"
description: "Switch to another character, or leave the server and save."
---

## Switching characters

Type `/relog` to go back to the character list. Your place, your health, your bag, and your guns are saved first. You cannot switch while you are dead, tied, cuffed, or in a trade. Wait before you switch again.

## Leaving

Type `/logout` to save and return to the RedM menu. You cannot leave while you are dead, tied, cuffed, or in a trade, or right after a switch. Quit in the F8 console closes the game without that save.
