---
title: "Shops"
description: "Walk up to a clerk, buy and sell, and finish the errand on Work."
---

Walk up to the clerk and press G. A short pause plays while the counter is gathered, then it opens. If someone is already at that counter, it does not open, and the line says someone is already there. The Shop prompt goes away while the counter is open. Press Escape, or press Close, to leave it.

The counter shows only what was saved for that shop. An empty shelf has nothing to buy or sell.

Drag, split, use, give, and drop work the same way they do when you open the [bag](/player/satchel) on its own.

## On the map

The store mark shows on the map while you are near the shop. Walk away and it leaves the map until you come back. The town mark stays on the map. Rest on it to see which shops that town has.

## Other counters

The gunsmith, the butcher, the trapper, the bait shop in Lagras, the doctor, the saloon, and the barber each keep their own counter. It shows only what was saved for that shop. A pile above your standing stays locked. A haircut is not on the counter yet.

## The counter

Rest the pointer on a pile to see its name, what it is, and the price. A trade the clerk will take instead of dollars is listed there too. A pile that needs a higher standing says so. Drag something into Want to ask for it. Drag Dollars onto Offer to pay. Shift-click a pile to move all of it. If Want already has a purchase, Shift-click on dollars or the trade goods moves only what that purchase still needs. Ctrl-click moves 10. A shop pile that does not run out has no whole pile, so Shift leaves it and Ctrl still takes 10. Click a pile on Want or Offer to put that whole pile back. The dollar stack drops by that many as soon as they land, including dollars in a pouch you are still carrying. A pile the clerk will not buy is grey and stays in your bag. Settle spends the price and puts the rest back in your bag. Drag the dollars off Offer, or close the counter, and they come back.

## Work

Press Work, beside Goods. Worths in Valentine has one errand. Go to the station platform, pick up the order slip, and hand it in at the station office. The slip stays in your satchel until you hand it over. Then bring four tins of beans back to Worths and turn them in on Work. Bought tins count, and they have to be in your satchel, not a pouch. Sweep the porch when he asks. Finishing that makes the clerk recognize you, and he will buy what you bring. Buying something also adds a little standing with that shop. Every shop adds the same amount.
