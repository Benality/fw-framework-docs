The kit button on the left of the [satchel](/player/satchel) opens a row of slots under the grid. It stays closed until you press that button. The Kit filter keeps your weapons and your ammo on the grid.

The slots are the right revolver, the left revolver, a long gun, a bow, a lasso, a knife, a lantern, binoculars, and a fishing pole. Equipping takes the item out of the bag and puts it on a slot. Unequipping puts it back.

A sidearm needs a gunbelt, and so does the knife. The left revolver also needs the offhand holster, and the right revolver has to be filled first. Dragging a gun onto the empty left slot shows `Equip the right revolver first.` Taking the right revolver off while the left one is still there shows `Take the left revolver off first.`

If the gunbelt comes off, the right revolver, the left revolver, and the knife all go back to the bag. If just the offhand holster comes off, only the left revolver goes back. If the bag is full, you see `Make room in the satchel first.`

A gun on the belt is not in the satchel, so it is not part of a [trade](/player/trade). Put it in the bag first.

Right-click a filled loadout slot for Inspect and Clean. Inspect draws the gun and plays the inspect hold, then the bag closes. A knife, lasso, lantern, binoculars, bow, or fishing pole cannot be inspected this way. Clean does nothing yet.

## Ammunition

The gunbelt stays closed until you press its button. It closes when the bag closes. Each kind on the character is an icon with the count in the corner. Rest on an icon to read the name. A kind at zero is left off. Nothing on the belt shows `Nothing on the belt.`

Drag a box or a bundle of arrows from the bag onto the gunbelt to load one. You can also use the box from the bag. That puts rounds on the character. The bag stays open. A box shows the revolver-cylinder loading prompt at the bottom right for about two seconds. Backspace closes the bag. A box that is still opening is cancelled, and the box stays in the bag. Arrows apply right away. If that kind is already at its limit, the item is not used, and you see `You cannot carry any more of that ammunition.`

Those rounds stay on the character. Draw the gun and press reload. Firing spends them. The bag is not charged again. Two guns that take the same rounds, such as two Cattlemen, share that pool. One revolver box adds 12 rounds, up to 200.

Drag that kind's icon from the gunbelt onto the satchel, or right-click it and choose Unload, to put one full box back in the bag. Those rounds come off that kind only. The bag stays open. If fewer than a full box are left, the menu says Empty. Empty clears the leftover and does not give an item, so a half-used box cannot be turned back into a full one. Dragging a short stack onto the satchel shows `Not a full box.` and leaves the rounds in place. If the bag cannot hold the box, nothing changes and you see `You cannot carry any more of those.`

Each kind has its own limit. Taking express revolver rounds off frees room for more express rounds. It does not raise the limit on regular revolver rounds. When a kind hits zero it drops off the weapon wheel and off the gunbelt.
