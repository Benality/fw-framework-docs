---
title: "HUD"
description: "How to show, hide, and rearrange the health and stamina cores."
---

While you are playing a character, the custom cores sit on the screen. They replace the game's own cores for health and stamina. When you are on a horse, horse health and stamina show as well.

Type `/hud` to hide the cores. Type `/hud` again to bring them back.

Type `/hudsettings` to rearrange them. Drag a core on the screen. Use the panel to change size, color, shape, and whether it shows. Close the satchel and the shop first, so the mouse can reach the panel.

Reset puts the default layout back in the editor. Cancel closes without saving. Save keeps the layout for your account, so it is still there after you leave and come back.
