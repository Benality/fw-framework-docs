---
title: "HUD"
description: "How to show, hide, and rearrange the health and stamina cores."
---

While you are playing a character, the custom cores sit on the screen. They replace the game's own cores for health and stamina. Hunger and thirst show beside them. When you are on a horse, horse health and stamina show as well.

The small map stays hidden. Hold or press Alt to show it for a few seconds, then it hides again.

Type `/hud` to hide the cores. Type `/hud` again to bring them back.

Type `/hudsettings` to rearrange them. Drag the settings panel by its title to move it. Drag a core on the screen to place it. Use the panel to change size, color, shape, and whether it shows. Close the satchel and the shop first, so the mouse can reach the panel.

Reset puts the default layout back in the editor. Cancel closes without saving. Save keeps the layout for your account, so it is still there after you leave and come back.
