---
title: "HUD"
description: "How to show, hide, and rearrange the cores, and how hunger, thirst, temperature, and food work."
---

While you are playing a character, the custom cores sit on the screen. They replace the game's own cores for health and stamina. Hunger, thirst, and temperature show beside them. When you are on a horse, horse health and stamina show as well.

The small map stays hidden. Press Alt to show it for a few seconds, then it hides again.

Hunger and thirst slowly fall as you walk, run, or ride. Eat and drink from your satchel to fill them. If either reaches empty, you start losing health until you eat or drink again.

Temperature follows the weather, your clothes, and whether you are in water. The icon shows the degrees. Cold weather makes hunger fall faster. Heat makes thirst fall faster. Freezing or burning chips your health until you find shelter, change clothes, or use something that warms or cools you.

Running hard drains the outer stamina ring. When that ring empties, the inner stamina core drops. Standing still fills the outer ring again. The inner core only comes back when you rest well fed, or when you use food, drink, or tonics that restore it.

Type `/hud` to hide the cores. Type `/hud` again to bring them back.

Type `/hudsettings` to rearrange them. Drag the settings panel by its title to move it. Drag a core on the screen to place it. Close the satchel and the shop first, so the mouse can reach the panel.

The **General** tab is for every core at once: scale, opacity, grid snap, shape (circle, square, or hexagon), the default ring color, and whether a ring appears on the ground when you change voice range. Each other tab is one icon. There you can hide it, change its size, split the outer ring into segments, show the number, and set that ring’s color. The picture inside each core stays the game art and does not recolor. The clock tab can hide the clock and change its text color. The voice tab moves or hides the microphone icon that shows whisper, normal, or shout.

Reset puts the default layout back in the editor. Cancel closes without saving. Save keeps the layout for your account, so it is still there after you leave and come back.
