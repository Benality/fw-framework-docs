---
title: "Satchel"
description: "Open the bag, use a pouch, open a parcel, and read a mark."
---

Press B, or type `/satchel`, to open your bag. Rest on an item to read its name. Right-click it to interact. B, Escape, or Backspace closes it.

The kit button on the left opens a row of slots under the grid. It stays closed until you press that button. The bottom row of the bag stays free, so you can still set something there. The Kit filter keeps your weapons and your ammo on the grid. The gunbelt is on the [weapons](/player/weapons) page.

## Pouches

The gunbelt and each pouch are their own window. Drag a title to move that window. Three pouches can be open together. The container filter on the left shows your pouches. Right-click a pouch and choose Open. A pouch cannot be put inside a chest or another pouch.

Drag something onto a pouch to put the whole pile inside. The pouch has to allow that kind of item, and it needs room. Split the pile first when you want to put in less.

Discard throws something away for good. Drop sets a pouch on the ground. Someone else can open it or pick it up.

## Dollars in the bag

The Currency filter shows your dollars. Dollars in a pouch you are still carrying still spend. A bag on the ground, or a chest, does not, until you take the dollars out. One pile holds up to 500.

Right-click a pile and choose Split in half, or Split amount and slide to how many you want. The new pile needs an empty square. Drag one pile onto another to combine them. Do that when you want to give some, [trade](/player/trade) some, or put some in a money bag. Drop that bag to set the dollars on the ground. Discard leaves the dollars in your bag. Take the dollars out of a money bag before you throw the bag away.

Giving a pile, and trading, are on the [trade](/player/trade) page. Spending them is on [money](/player/money).

## Parcels

A parcel opens like a pouch. Drag what you want into your bag. When the last thing leaves, the parcel is gone. Discard throws the parcel away, and anything still inside goes with it. You cannot set a parcel on the ground. Some parcels can be given to someone nearby. The rest stay with you.

## Marks

Some items have a colored frame. Green is uncommon, blue is rare, purple is epic, and gold is legendary. Rest on the item and the word is under the name. A mission item has a frame too. It stays in your satchel. You cannot give it, trade it, sell it, throw it away, or put it in a pouch.
