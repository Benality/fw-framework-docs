---
title: "Money"
description: "Dollars in the satchel. Split a pile, carry it in a pouch, or spend it."
---

The dollars are a real stack in the satchel. One pile holds at most 500 dollars. A larger amount is several piles.

You can split a pile, [give](/player/trade) that pile, offer it in a [trade](/player/trade), or drag it into a pouch whose categories include currency. The money bag item accepts currency. A herb pouch does not. Dollars in a pouch you are still carrying still spend.

Drop is refused on the dollars themselves. Put them in a money bag, then drop the bag. The dollars stay inside that bag. Picking the bag up puts them back in what you can spend.

Discard is refused on the dollars. Discard is also refused on a bag that still holds dollars. The reason is `Empty the bag first.` Take the dollars out, or drop the bag.

A bag on the ground keeps its own dollars. A chest does too. Those dollars do not spend until you take them out.

At a [shop](/player/shops), drag Dollars onto Offer to pay. Settle spends the price and puts the rest back in your bag. Drag the dollars off Offer, or close the counter, and they come back.
