---
title: "Player guide"
description: "What you press, and what happens. Type /guide in the game to open the same help."
---

Type `/guide` to open help. Type `/guide` again, or press Escape, to close it.

The first subject is the large card on the left. The next ones fill the cards on the right. The line along the bottom describes the card you are on. Enter opens that subject. Escape steps back. On the front page, Escape closes help.

These pages are the subjects that are written. Each one says what you do.

- [Satchel](/player/satchel) — the bag, pouches, parcels, and marks.
- [Trade](/player/trade) — offer a trade, or give one pile.
- [Money](/player/money) — dollars in the bag.
- [Shops](/player/shops) — the counter, and the errand on Work.
- [Weapons](/player/weapons) — the kit, the gunbelt, and ammunition.
- [Characters](/player/characters) — switch characters, or leave.

A script that opens this screen uses [fw_guide](/api/fw-guide).
