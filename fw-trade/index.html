    fw_trade¶

    Runs on the server and in the player's game. Two players trade what is in their satchels. The bag screen from fw_satchel draws it. This page is how a trade starts, how the two offers lock, and how the items move.

    Money is not part of a trade. A gun on the belt is not in the satchel, so it is not part of a trade either. Put it in the bag first. That is on fw_weapons.

    Starting a trade¶

    Stand within about 8 meters of the other players. Hold the interact control (the same one a clothing shop uses) and the prompt says Offer trade. Typing /trade does the same thing.

    That opens markers on the nearby players. Click the person you want. Escape cancels and nothing is offered. Someone in another building does not get a marker. Up to 8 people nearby can show at once.

    The other player gets a prompt, {Name} wants to trade. Hold to open it. Press the other control to decline. The prompt lasts about 20 seconds. If it runs out, both players see The trade offer expired.

    The name is the character's first and last name. source on this page is the player's number for this visit. nil means nothing came back.

    Type /tradeui to open this screen by yourself. Your satchel is on the right. Their offer fills with copies of what is already in your bag, so you can see that grid. Drag into your offer, then lock it. Accept turns on, and clicking it does not move anything. Escape, Decline, or /tradeui again closes it.

    A trade does not start when either player is already trading, dead, or not playing a character. The player who offered it then sees a short reason, such as They are too far away. or They are already trading.

    The screen¶

    Both players see the same bag screen.

    • The right side is their own satchel.
    • Your offer is the top grid on the left. It is 10 cells across and 5 down. Drag a whole placement up from the satchel. Drag it back to take it out of the offer. The placement stays in the satchel until the trade is accepted, and it still takes up its old space there.
    • Their offer is the bottom grid. It stays empty, with They have not locked an offer., until the other player locks.
    • Decline trade is there for both players the whole time. B, Escape, Backspace, or Tab does the same thing. Both screens close and nothing moves.
    • Accept trade is only on the screen of the player who offered the trade.

    Lock offer shows your grid to the other player and stops you from dragging. Unlock offer hides it again and turns Accept off. You cannot drag while you are locked. Unlock first, then change the offer.

    Accept stays dim until both offers are locked. The other player never gets an Accept button. They see Waiting for them to accept. once both are locked. If either player unlocks, Accept turns off again.

    The offer grids only hold what fits in 10 by 5. A placement that does not fit stays in the satchel, and the player sees That does not fit.

    Drop, use, equip, and sort will not take a placement that is in an offer. The player sees That item is in a trade. Move and split still work on what is left in the bag, so a stack can be split and then dragged in. A pouch cannot be opened while the trade screen is up. The reason is Finish the trade first.

    When it finishes¶

    Accept gives each locked offer to the other satchel. A pouch keeps what is inside it, because that stays with the pouch. The items land in the first open cells. They do not keep the cells from the offer grid.

    If the other satchel cannot hold what is coming in, both players stay on the trade screen and see There is no room for that in the satchel. Nothing is taken.

    The trade also ends, and nothing moves, when someone walks farther than about 12 meters away, dies, leaves, or drops their character. Both players see a short reason, such as You walked too far apart.

    Giving an item¶

    Right-click a placement in the satchel and choose Give. The bag hides, the same markers come up, and you click who receives it. The whole stack moves. There is no trade screen and they do not have to accept. Split the stack first when you want to hand over only part of it.

    Both players get a line in the log, such as You gave Rope to {Name}. and {Name} gave you Rope. The same log is where a failure shows up, such as They have no room for that. Open the client console with F8 to read it. The server window shows that same line, with the player's number for this visit.

    Nothing moves, and you see a short reason, when they are too far away, already trading, or their satchel has no room. You see They have no room for that. in that last case. You also cannot give while you are already in a trade. The reason is Finish the trade first.

    Escape on the markers puts the bag back and leaves the item where it was.

    Calls¶

    SelectPlayer¶

    Runs in the player's game. Shows the nearby-player markers and waits until they click one, or cancel. The player's number for this visit comes back. Nothing comes back when they cancel, nobody is close enough, or the markers are already up.

    --- The server id of the player they clicked. Nothing when they cancel.
---@return integer|nil serverId
exports.fw_trade:SelectPlayer()

    local serverId = exports.fw_trade:SelectPlayer()
if serverId == nil then
    return
end

    Call it from the player's game, not the server. It takes the mouse until they click or press Escape. Do not call it while another menu already has the mouse.

    IsInTrade¶

    Runs on the server. Yes when this player has the trade screen open. No during the prompt before that, and no when they are not trading.

    --- True when this player has the trade screen open.
---@param source integer
---@return boolean
exports.fw_trade:IsInTrade(source)

    if exports.fw_trade:IsInTrade(source) then
    print('Finish the trade first.')
    return
end

    The swap itself is CommitTrade on fw_inventory. SetHold on that page is how an offer stays in the satchel until Accept. Another script should not call those to finish a trade of its own unless it is running the same lock rules. This resource already does.

    Messages¶

    A message on the server is heard with AddEventHandler. A message from the server to the player's game is heard with RegisterNetEvent. A message from the player's game to the server is sent with TriggerServerEvent. Your script hears one only if it is already running. Put the listener at the top of your script.

    These are sent by this resource's own screen. Do not send them from another script. That skips the distance check, or fights the two players for the same trade.

    From the player's game to the server¶

    Message What it does
    fw_trade:request Offers a trade to the player number they clicked.
    fw_trade:give Hands one whole placement to that player. The arguments are their player number and the placement id. The bag screen sends this after Give.
    fw_trade:respond The other player accepts (true) or declines (false) the prompt.
    fw_trade:offer Replaces that player's unlocked offer. The argument is a list of { id, x, y, rotated }.
    fw_trade:lock true locks the offer. false unlocks it.
    fw_trade:accept The player who started the trade finishes it. Refused until both offers are locked.
    fw_trade:decline Either player cancels. Nothing moves.
    fw_trade:abort The player's game reports that someone walked too far or died. The server checks the distance and whether they are dead before it ends the trade.

    From the server to the player's game¶

    Message What it carries
    fw_trade:waiting The other character's name, while the prompt is up.
    fw_trade:invited { from, name } for the player who was offered the trade. from is that other player's number.
    fw_trade:inviteClosed A short reason. The trade screen does not open.
    fw_trade:state The current view: whether you started it, the other name, both lock flags, your offer, and their offer once they have locked.
    fw_trade:notice A short reason while the screen stays open, such as There is no room for that in the satchel.
    fw_trade:closed A short reason. The bag closes.
    fw_trade:rejected A short reason when the offer never started. 
    RegisterNetEvent('fw_trade:closed', function(reason)
    print(reason)
end)
